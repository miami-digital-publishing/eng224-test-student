# Playing Alone {.concept}

In a solo game, you compete against the **rival captain**, a ship controlled by a deck of cards. The rival doesn't buy or sell cargo. Instead, it races you for contracts and docks.

## How to win a solo game

You win if you have more renown than the rival at the end of the eighth round. For a harder challenge, try to beat the rival by 10 or more renown.

## Differences from the normal game

- The sky map uses the two-player layout.
- You start with 12 coins.
- The rival captain takes its turn after each of your actions.

To get started, see [Set up a solo game](/topics/solo/set-up-solo.md).
