# Play the Rival's Turn {.task}

After each of your actions, play one rival action.

1. Reveal the top card of the rival deck.
2. Move the rival's airship toward the target shown on the card.
    - The rival moves up to 3 regions, ignoring the wind.
3. If the rival reaches its target, do the action shown on the card.
    - **Contract:** discard the open contract at that island and add its renown to the rival's score.
    - **Dock:** place a rival dock at that island, if there isn't one already.
    - **Raid:** you lose 2 coins if your ship is in the same region.
4. Place the card face up in the rival discard pile.
5. If the rival deck is empty, shuffle the discard pile to make a new deck.

The rival never pays wages, collects landing fees, or rides out storms.
