# The Rival Captain {.concept}

The rival captain is a simple automated opponent. It never holds cargo or coins. Each rival card tells it where to sail and what to do when it gets there.

## What the rival wants

The rival always heads toward the open contract with the highest renown value. When it arrives, it takes the contract, whether or not it has the goods, and scores its renown. This means you have to race the rival to the best contracts.

## Rival docks

Some rival cards tell the rival to build a dock. The rival builds for free, and its docks charge you landing fees just like another player's docks.

## The rival and the weather

The rival ignores fog, but it can't enter the storm region. If a rival card would move it into the storm, it stops in the region before.
