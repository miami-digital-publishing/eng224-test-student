# Action Summary {.reference}

On your turn, choose one of these actions. You'll take three actions each round.

| Action | Where | Cost | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Sail](/topics/actions/sail.md) | Anywhere | None | Move up to your speed |
| [Load cargo](/topics/actions/load-cargo.md) | An island that sells goods | Market price | Cargo in your hold |
| [Sell cargo](/topics/actions/sell-cargo.md) | An island that buys goods | None | Coins |
| [Fulfill a contract](/topics/actions/fulfill-contract.md) | The contract's island | The listed goods | Renown and coins |
| [Hire crew](/topics/actions/hire-crew.md) | Any island with a dock | 3 to 6 coins | A crew card |
| [Upgrade your airship](/topics/actions/upgrade-airship.md) | Any island | 6 to 8 coins | +1 to a stat, 1 renown |
| [Build a dock](/topics/actions/build-dock.md) | An island without your dock | 5 coins | 2 renown, landing fees |
| [Rest](/topics/actions/rest.md) | Anywhere | None | 2 coins, repair 1 hull |
