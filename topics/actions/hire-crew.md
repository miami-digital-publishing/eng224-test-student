# Hire Crew {.task}

Crew members give your ship special abilities. You can hire them at any island that has a dock, including another captain's dock.

1. Choose a card from the crew market.
2. Pay the cost printed on the card.
    - If you're hiring at your own dock, pay 1 coin less.
3. Place the card in an empty crew berth on your airship board.
    - If all four berths are full, discard one of your crew first.
4. Leave the empty space in the crew market. It's refilled at dusk.

To learn what each crew member does, see [About crew](/topics/cards/crew/about-crew.md).
