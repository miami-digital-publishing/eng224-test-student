# Upgrade Your Airship {.task}

An upgrade permanently improves one of your ship's stats.

1. Choose a stat to upgrade.
    - Each stat can be upgraded twice.
2. Pay the cost to the bank.
    - Sails cost 8 coins, hold costs 6 coins, and hull costs 7 coins.
3. Take the matching upgrade tile and place it in the next empty slot on your airship board.
4. Move your marker forward 1 space on the renown track.

| Upgrade | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Sails | 8 coins | +1 speed |
| Hold | 6 coins | +2 cargo spaces |
| Hull | 7 coins | +1 hull |
