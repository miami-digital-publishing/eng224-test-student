# Sell Cargo {.task}

You can sell cargo at any island that buys that good.

1. Choose one kind of good in your hold that the island buys.
2. Count how many tokens of that good you're selling.
3. Take coins from the bank: the good's base price plus 2, for each token.
    - At Windhollow, take 1 coin less per token.
4. Return the sold tokens to the general supply, not to the island's market.

At Cloudmarket, you may sell two kinds of goods in one action.

> **Note:** You can't sell goods to an island that sells the same good. Greenterrace, for example, will never buy tea.
