# Load Cargo {.task}

You can load cargo at any island that sells goods.

1. Choose a good that the island sells.
2. Check the market price.
    - The price is the good's base price, plus 1 coin for each empty space in that market.
3. Pay the price to the bank for each token you load.
4. Move the tokens from the island's market to your hold.
    - You can't load more tokens than your hold has room for.
5. Repeat for a second good, if the island sells more than one.

You can load as many tokens as you can afford and carry in one action.
