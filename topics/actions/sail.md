# Sail {.task}

Sailing moves your airship from region to region along the air lanes.

1. Check your ship's speed on your airship board.
2. Check the wind compass.
    - Sailing **with** the wind costs 0 speed for that move.
    - Sailing **against** the wind costs 2 speed for that move.
    - Sailing across the wind costs the normal 1 speed.
3. Move your airship one region at a time, along air lanes, until you've used your speed or you choose to stop.
    - You can't enter the storm region. See [Storms](/topics/weather/storms.md).
    - Entering a fog bank ends your movement. See [Fog banks](/topics/weather/fog-banks.md).
    ```
    speed = 2
    ```

4. Choose which island in the region your ship lands at.
5. If the island has another captain's dock, pay that captain 1 coin.

![The wind rose, showing how much speed each direction costs](/images/wind-rose.svg)

> **Tip:** A ship with speed 2 can cross almost the entire map in a single action if it sails with the wind the whole way.
