# Build a Dock {.task}

Docks earn you money whenever other captains land at your island, and each is worth renown at the end of the game.

1. Check that the island you're at doesn't already have your dock.
    - An island can have docks from several different captains.
2. Pay 5 coins to the bank.
    - At Hollowrock, pay 7 coins.
3. Place one of your dock markers beside the island tile.
4. Move your marker forward 2 spaces on the renown track.

Each captain has only three docks. Choose your islands carefully: the busiest islands are usually near the center of the map.
