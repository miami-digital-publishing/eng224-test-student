# Fulfill a Contract {.task}

Contracts are your biggest source of renown. To fulfill one, you must deliver the listed goods to the listed island.

![A contract card, showing its renown value, required goods, and destination](/images/contract-card.svg)

1. Sail to the island named on the contract.
2. Check that your hold contains every good the contract lists.
3. Return those goods from your hold to the general supply.
4. Take the coin reward shown on the card from the bank.
5. Move your marker forward on the renown track by the card's renown value.
    - At Brightspire, add 1 extra renown.
6. Place the contract face up in front of you.

You can fulfill either a contract you're holding or the face-up contract beside the island you're at.
