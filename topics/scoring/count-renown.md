# Count Your Renown {.task}

Most renown is added to the track during the game. At the end, add the bonuses that are only scored once.

1. Check that every captain's marker on the renown track is correct.
    - Add up the renown on their fulfilled contracts, plus 2 for each dock and 1 for each upgrade.
2. Each captain with one of each crew type adds 4 renown.
3. Each captain adds 1 renown for every 10 coins they have.
    - Round down. For example, 27 coins are worth 2 renown.
4. Each captain who built all three of their docks adds 3 renown.
5. Compare the final scores.

The captain with the most renown wins. If there's a tie, see [Breaking ties](/topics/scoring/breaking-ties.md).
