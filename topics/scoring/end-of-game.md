# End of the Game {.concept}

The game ends after the dusk phase of the eighth round. (In a five-player game, it ends after the seventh round, because the round tracker started on space 2.)

## Last chances

Captains know exactly when the game will end, so the last round is often the busiest. Contracts left in your hand at the end of the game are worth nothing, so try to fulfill them before the final dusk.

## What doesn't count

- Cargo left in your hold is worth nothing.
- Contracts you haven't fulfilled are worth nothing.
- Damage to your hull doesn't cost renown.

When the game ends, [count your renown](/topics/scoring/count-renown.md).
