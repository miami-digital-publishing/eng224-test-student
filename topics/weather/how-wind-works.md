# How Wind Works {.concept}

The wind is the most important force in Skyharbor. It decides which routes are fast and which are slow.

![The wind rose, showing how much speed each direction costs](/images/wind-rose.svg)

## The wind compass

At the start of every round, the first captain rolls the wind die and turns the wind compass to match. The arrow on the compass shows which way the wind is blowing for the whole round.

## Sailing with and against the wind

Each move from one region to the next costs speed:

- **With the wind** (in the same direction as the arrow): 0 speed.
- **Across the wind** (sideways to the arrow): 1 speed.
- **Against the wind** (toward the arrow): 2 speed.

This means a slow ship can travel a long way if the wind is right, and a fast ship can get stuck if it isn't.

## Trade winds

Some air lanes have a trade wind arrow printed on them. Sailing in the direction of a trade wind always costs 0 speed, whichever way the compass points.
