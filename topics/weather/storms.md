# Storms {.concept}

A storm is a region of the sky so dangerous that no captain will sail into it on purpose.

## The storm marker

The gray cloud marker shows where the storm is. No airship can sail into the storm region. If the storm moves into a region where an airship already is, that captain must [ride out the storm](/topics/weather/ride-out-storm.md).

## How the storm moves

The storm moves only when the wind die shows the storm face. It moves one region in the direction of the current wind. Some event cards also move the storm.

## Safe harbors

Storms can never enter the Ashfall region, because the volcano's heat breaks up the clouds. Some captains build docks at Ashfall for exactly this reason.

> **Tip:** Watch the wind compass. If the storm is one region upwind of you, there's a one-in-six chance it will reach you next round.
