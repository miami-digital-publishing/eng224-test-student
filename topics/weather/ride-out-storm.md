# Ride Out a Storm {.task}

If the storm moves into your region, you must ride it out before the action phase begins.

1. Choose whether to protect your hull or your cargo.
2. If you protect your hull, discard half of your cargo, rounded up.
    - You choose which tokens to discard.
3. If you protect your cargo, lose 1 hull.
    - Move your hull marker down one space.
4. If your hull reaches 0, your ship must limp home.
    - Move your airship to any island with your dock, or to Windhollow if you have no docks.
    - Your hull returns to 1.
5. Sail out of the storm region with your first action.
    - Leaving a storm region costs 1 extra speed.

A Navigator can help you avoid storms; see [Navigator](/topics/cards/crew/navigator.md).
