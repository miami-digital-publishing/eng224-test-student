# Fog Banks {.concept}

Fog banks appear when certain event cards are revealed. Place a fog token on the region the card names.

## Sailing into fog

A ship that sails into a region with a fog token must stop there, even if it has speed left. You can still take other actions in a fog bank, such as Load, Sell, or Rest.

## Sailing out of fog

Leaving a fog bank costs the normal amount of speed. A captain with a Lookout ignores fog completely.

## Clearing fog

Fog tokens are removed at dusk in the round after they appear, unless an event card says otherwise.
