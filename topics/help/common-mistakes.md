# Common Mistakes {.concept}

These are the rules new players most often get wrong.

## Forgetting to pay wages

Crew members cost 1 coin each at every dusk. Players often hire three crew members in their first two rounds and then can't pay them.

## Selling where the good is sold

An island never buys a good it sells. You can't buy tea at Greenterrace and sell it back there.

## Moving against the wind

Sailing against the wind costs **2** speed, not 1. A ship with speed 2 can only make one move against the wind.

## Adding renown at the end instead of during the game

Docks, upgrades, and contracts score as soon as you get them. Move your marker right away, so you don't forget.

## Taking three actions in a row

Captains take **one** action at a time, then the next captain goes. See [Action phase](/topics/turns/action-phase.md).
