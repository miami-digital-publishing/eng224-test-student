# Frequently Asked Questions {.concept}

## Can two airships be in the same region?

Yes. Any number of airships can share a region, and even the same island. Only the starting islands must be different.

## Can I sail through a region without landing at an island?

Yes. You only choose an island in the region where you stop. You pay landing fees only at the island where you land.

## What happens if I can't afford my crew's wages?

The crew members you can't pay leave immediately. You choose which ones stay.

## Can I fulfill a contract with goods I just loaded at the same island?

Yes, as long as you take two actions: one to load the goods and one to fulfill the contract.

## A component is missing. What can I use instead?

Use coins from another game for missing coins, and small colored beads for missing cargo tokens. For a missing card, write the card's details from the [Event card list](/topics/cards/event-card-list.md) on a blank card.

## Does the rival captain pay landing fees?

No. See [Play the rival's turn](/topics/solo/rival-turn.md).
