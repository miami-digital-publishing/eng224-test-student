# Quick Reference {.reference}

## A round

| Phase | Steps |
|---|---|
| Dawn | Reveal event, roll wind, move storm if needed |
| Action | Three actions each, one at a time, clockwise |
| Dusk | Pay wages, refill markets and crew, pass compass |

## Sailing costs

| Direction | Speed used |
|---|---|
| With the wind or a trade wind | 0 |
| Across the wind | 1 |
| Against the wind | 2 |
| Leaving a storm region | +1 |

## Costs

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Dock | 5 coins (7 at Hollowrock) |
| Sails upgrade | 8 coins |
| Hold upgrade | 6 coins |
| Hull upgrade | 7 coins |
| Crew | 3 to 6 coins, 1 less at your own dock |
| Landing at another captain's dock | 1 coin |
