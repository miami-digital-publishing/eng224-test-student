# Glossary {.reference}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Air lane | A dotted line connecting two regions. Ships can only sail along air lanes. |
| Captain | A player. |
| Crew market | The row of face-up crew cards available to hire. |
| Dock | A marker that earns you landing fees and renown. |
| First captain | The player holding the gold compass, who takes the first action each round. |
| Fog bank | A region with a fog token. Ships that enter must stop. |
| Hold | The number of cargo tokens your ship can carry. |
| Hull | How much storm damage your ship can take. |
| Landing fee | The 1 coin you pay when you land at another captain's dock. |
| Open contract | A face-up contract beside an island that any captain can fulfill. |
| Region | An area of sky on the map, holding one or two islands. |
| Renown | Victory points. The captain with the most renown wins. |
| Rival captain | The automated opponent in solo play. |
| Speed | How many regions your ship can move in one Sail action. |
| Storm | The region marked by the storm marker. Ships can't enter it. |
| Trade wind | A printed arrow on an air lane. Sailing with it costs no speed. |
