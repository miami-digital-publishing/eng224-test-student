# About Tic-Tac-Toe {.concept}

Tic-Tac-Toe is a quick game for two players. Players take turns marking spaces on a three-by-three grid, trying to be the first to get three of their marks in a row.

## Who should play

Tic-Tac-Toe is good for players of all ages. A game usually takes less than a minute, so it works well as a warm-up or a tie-breaker.

## The goal

Be the first player to place three of your marks in a straight line: across, down, or diagonally.
