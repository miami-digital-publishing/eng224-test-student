# Action Phase {.concept}

The action phase is where most of the game happens. Starting with the first captain and going clockwise, each captain takes **one action** at a time. Play continues around the table until every captain has taken three actions.

## Taking turns one action at a time

Because you take one action and then pass, other captains can react to what you do. If you're sailing toward Loomhold to buy silk, another captain may get there first and empty the market. Plan for that.

## Choosing actions

You can take the same action more than once in a round. For example, you can Sail, Sail, and Sell. There are eight actions to choose from; see the [Action summary](/topics/actions/action-summary.md).

## Free actions

Some things don't count as an action:

- making change with the bank
- discarding cargo you don't want
- reading a contract card or the rules
- using a crew ability that says **Free**

## Passing

If you can't or don't want to take an action, you may pass. A captain who passes gains 1 coin, and still takes their remaining actions later in the round.
