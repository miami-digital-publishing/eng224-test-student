# Round Overview {.concept}

A game of Skyharbor lasts eight rounds. Every round has the same three phases, always in the same order.

| Phase | Who acts | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| [Dawn](/topics/turns/dawn-phase.md) | First captain | Reveal an event and roll the wind |
| [Action](/topics/turns/action-phase.md) | Everyone, one at a time | Each captain takes three actions |
| [Dusk](/topics/turns/dusk-phase.md) | Everyone, at once | Pay crew, refill markets, pass the compass |

## Why the order matters

The wind is rolled at dawn, before anyone moves. That means every captain knows the wind before planning their actions. Event cards, however, sometimes change things in the middle of the action phase, so read each event carefully.

## Keeping track

Move the round tracker forward at the end of every round. When the tracker would move past space 8, the game ends. See [End of the game](/topics/scoring/end-of-game.md).
