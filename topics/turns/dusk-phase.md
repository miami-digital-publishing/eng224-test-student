# Dusk Phase {.task}

All captains do the dusk phase together.

1. Pay each of your crew members 1 coin.
    - If you can't pay a crew member, they leave. Discard their card.
2. Refill each island market up to three tokens of each good it sells.
    - If the supply runs out, refill the islands in order, starting with region 1.
3. Refill the crew market to four cards.
4. Replace any contract that was completed this round with a new card from the deck.
5. Pass the gold compass token to the left.

Then [end the round](/topics/turns/end-of-round.md).
