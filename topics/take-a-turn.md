# Take a turn {.task}

Players alternate turns until someone wins or the grid is full.

1. Choose any empty square on the grid.
2. Place one of your markers in that square.
3. Check whether you have three marks in a row.
    - If you do, you win the game.
    - If you don't, your opponent takes their turn.

> **Note:** Once a marker is placed, it cannot be moved.
