# Coins {.reference}

Coins are the money of Skyharbor. Keep your coins in front of you where other players can see them.

| Coin | Value | Quantity in the box |
|---|---|---|
| Copper | 1 | 40 |
| Silver | 5 | 25 |
| Gold | 10 | 15 |

## Starting coins

| Players | Each captain starts with |
|---|---|
| 1 (solo) | 12 coins |
| 2 | 10 coins |
| 3 or 4 | 8 coins |
| 5 | 7 coins |

You may make change from the bank at any time. If the bank runs out of coins, use any other small items as replacements.
