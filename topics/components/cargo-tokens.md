# Cargo Tokens {.reference}

There are four kinds of goods. Each has its own color and shape.

| Good | Color | Shape | Base price | Sold at |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tea | Green | Leaf | 2 coins | Greenterrace, Brightspire, Saltmere, Stillwater |
| Silk | Purple | Spool | 3 coins | Loomhold, Brightspire, Cloudmarket |
| Spice | Orange | Jar | 4 coins | Coralreach, Ashfall, Saltmere, Emberlight |
| Iron | Gray | Ingot | 3 coins | Deepvein, Ashfall, Hollowrock, Cloudmarket |

The base price is what an island charges when its market is full. Prices rise as the market empties; see [Load cargo](/topics/actions/load-cargo.md).

> **Note:** Cargo tokens are limited. If the supply of a good runs out, nobody can load that good until someone sells it back.
