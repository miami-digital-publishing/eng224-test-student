# Airship Boards {.concept}

Each captain has an airship board. It shows your ship's three **stats**, your cargo hold, and spaces for crew and upgrades.

![An airship board with the speed, hold, and hull tracks](/images/airship-board.svg)

## The three stats

- **Speed** is how many regions you can sail in one Sail action. Every ship starts with speed 2.
- **Hold** is how many cargo tokens your ship can carry. Every ship starts with a hold of 3.
- **Hull** is how much storm damage your ship can take before it must return to port. Every ship starts with hull 2.

## Upgrade slots

Each stat has two empty upgrade slots. When you upgrade your airship, place the upgrade tile in the next empty slot for that stat. A stat can't be upgraded more than twice. See [Upgrade your airship](/topics/actions/upgrade-airship.md).

## Crew berths

The bottom of the board has four crew berths. Each crew card you hire goes in one berth. If all four are full, you must dismiss a crew member before you can hire a new one.
