# The Sky Map {.concept}

The sky map is the main board. It shows seven **regions** of open sky, connected by **air lanes**.

![The sky map, with seven regions connected by air lanes](/images/sky-map.svg)

## Regions

Each region holds one or two island tiles. Your airship is always in exactly one region. Moving from one region to a connected region costs one point of speed.

## Air lanes

The dotted lines between regions are air lanes. You can only sail along an air lane. Some lanes have a small arrow printed on them; these are **trade winds**, and sailing in the direction of the arrow costs no speed at all.

## Tracks around the edge

- **The renown track** runs around the outside of the board. Move your dock marker along it whenever you gain renown, so everyone can see the score.
- **The round track** in the top corner shows which of the eight rounds you're in.
- **The wind compass** in the center shows which way the wind is blowing this round.
