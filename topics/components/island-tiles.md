# Island Tiles {.reference}

Each island tile shows the island's name, the goods it **sells** (on the left), and the goods it **buys** (on the right).

| Island | Sells | Buys | Special rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greenterrace | Tea | Iron, silk | None |
| Loomhold | Silk | Tea, spice | None |
| Coralreach | Spice | Iron | Sells spice at 1 coin less in round 1 |
| Deepvein | Iron | Tea, spice | None |
| Brightspire | Tea, silk | Spice | Contracts here are worth 1 extra renown |
| Ashfall | Iron, spice | Silk | Storms can't enter this region |
| Windhollow | Nothing | Everything | Market prices are 1 coin lower |
| Saltmere | Spice, tea | Iron | None |
| Hollowrock | Iron | Silk, tea | Docks here cost 2 extra coins |
| Cloudmarket | Silk, iron | Spice | You may sell two kinds of goods in one action |
| Emberlight | Spice | Tea | Five-player game only |
| Stillwater | Tea | Silk | Five-player game only |

To place the tiles, see [Set up the sky map](/topics/setup/set-up-sky-map.md).
