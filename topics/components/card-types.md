# Card Types {.reference}

Skyharbor has four decks of cards. Each has a different back.

| Deck | Back | Cards | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contracts | Blue, with a wax seal | 40 | Goals that earn renown when you deliver goods |
| Events | Red, with a lightning bolt | 30 | Surprises revealed at the start of each round |
| Crew | Green, with a ship's wheel | 24 | Crew members with special abilities |
| Rival captain | Black, with a skull | 12 | Solo play only |
