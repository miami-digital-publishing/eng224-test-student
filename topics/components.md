# What's in the box {.reference}

You need only a few simple items to play.

| Item | Quantity | What it's for |
|------|----------|---------------|
| Game grid | 1 | The playing area: three rows of three squares |
| X markers | 5 | Player one's pieces |
| O markers | 4 | Player two's pieces |

You can also draw the grid on paper and use a pencil instead of markers.
