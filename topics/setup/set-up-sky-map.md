# Set Up the Sky Map {.task}

Set up the board before the players choose their colors. This takes about five minutes.

1. Unfold the sky map and place it in the middle of the table.
2. Remove the Emberlight and Stillwater island tiles from the game.
    - Skip this step in a five-player game. See [Setup for five players](/topics/setup/five-player-setup.md).
3. Shuffle the remaining island tiles face down.
4. Place one island tile face up on each numbered island space on the map.
    - Regions with two island spaces get two tiles.
5. Place the Windhollow tile in the center region, swapping it with whichever tile is there.
6. Fill each island's market with three cargo tokens of each good that island sells.
7. Shuffle the contract cards and place the deck beside the map.
    - Remove the ten advanced contracts first, unless you're playing the [Advanced contracts](/topics/variants/advanced-contracts.md) variant.
8. Deal one contract card face up beside each island tile.
9. Shuffle the event cards and place the deck face down beside the map.
10. Shuffle the crew cards and deal four face up in a row. This row is the **crew market**.
11. Place the storm marker on the region numbered 7.
12. Place the round tracker on space 1 of the round track.
13. Place the wind die and the fog tokens beside the board.

The sky map is ready. Next, [set up your player area](/topics/setup/set-up-player-area.md).
