# Setup for Five Players {.task}

A five-player game adds two islands and speeds up the end of the game. Follow the normal setup, with these changes.

1. Include the Emberlight and Stillwater island tiles when you shuffle the islands.
2. Place them on the two island spaces marked with a star.
3. Deal five cards to the crew market, instead of four.
4. Give each captain 7 starting coins.
5. Set the round tracker to space 2.
    - A five-player game lasts seven rounds.

> **Note:** With five players, the markets empty quickly. Watch the prices, and don't count on finding the goods you need at the island you're sailing toward.
