# Set Up Your Player Area {.task}

Each captain does these steps at the same time.

1. Choose a color and take the matching airship board, airship figure, and three dock markers.
2. Place your airship board in front of you.
3. Take your starting coins from the bank.
    - See the starting coins table in [Coins](/topics/components/coins.md).
4. Draw two contract cards and keep one.
    - Place the other at the bottom of the contract deck.
5. Place one of your dock markers on space 0 of the renown track.
6. Keep your other two dock markers in front of you. You'll build them during the game.

Your player area is ready. Next, [choose the first captain](/topics/setup/choose-first-captain.md).
