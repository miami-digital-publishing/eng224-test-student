# Engineer {.reference}

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Cost to hire | 6 coins |
| Wage | 1 coin each dusk |
| Ability | Upgrades cost 2 coins less |
| Repair ability | Free: repair 1 hull at the start of your first action each round |
| Copies in the deck | 6 |

Engineers are expensive, but captains who plan to upgrade all three stats should hire one early.
