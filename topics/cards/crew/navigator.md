# Navigator {.reference}

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Cost to hire | 5 coins |
| Wage | 1 coin each dusk |
| Ability | +1 speed on every Sail action |
| Storm ability | Free: once per round, move away from a storm before riding it out |
| Copies in the deck | 6 |

Navigators are most useful on large maps and for captains who rely on contracts at distant islands.
