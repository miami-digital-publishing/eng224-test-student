# About Crew {.concept}

Crew members are specialists who join your ship. Each one gives you an ability you can use for the rest of the game, as long as you keep paying them.

## The four crew types

There are four types of crew, with six cards of each type:

- [Navigator](/topics/cards/crew/navigator.md): helps you sail faster and avoid storms.
- [Quartermaster](/topics/cards/crew/quartermaster.md): gets you better prices.
- [Engineer](/topics/cards/crew/engineer.md): makes upgrades and repairs cheaper.
- [Lookout](/topics/cards/crew/lookout.md): sees through fog and spots open contracts.

## Wages

At every dusk, you pay each crew member 1 coin. If you can't pay, that crew member leaves.

## Full crew bonus

A captain who has one of each crew type at the end of the game earns 4 extra renown.
