# Event Cards {.concept}

Event cards bring surprises to every round. The first captain reveals one at the start of each dawn phase.

## Types of events

- **Now** events happen immediately and are then discarded.
- **Later** events stay face up and take effect at a time shown on the card, usually at dusk.
- **Round** events last for the whole round, then are discarded at the end of the round.

## Good news and bad news

About half the event cards help captains, for example by lowering prices or granting extra speed. The other half cause trouble, such as moving the storm, closing a market, or bringing fog.
