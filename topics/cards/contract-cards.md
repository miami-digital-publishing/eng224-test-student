# Contract Cards {.concept}

Contracts are orders posted by the island councils. Each one asks for a set of goods to be delivered to a particular island.

![A contract card, showing its renown value, required goods, and destination](/images/contract-card.svg)

## Reading a contract

- **Top left:** the renown value, from 2 to 7.
- **Top right:** the coin reward.
- **Middle:** the goods you must deliver.
- **Bottom:** the island where you must deliver them.

## Holding contracts

You can hold up to three contracts at once. Contracts in your hand are private; other captains can't see which goods you need.

## Open contracts

One contract sits face up beside each island. Any captain can fulfill an open contract. Open contracts are a race: if another captain fulfills it first, it's gone.

## Taking a new contract

When you fulfill a contract, you may draw a new one from the deck for free. You can also spend a Rest action to draw a contract instead of taking coins.
