# Game Overview {.concept}

Skyharbor is a trading and route-building game for two to five players, ages 10 and up. A game takes about 60 to 90 minutes. You can also play alone against a rival captain; see [Playing alone](/topics/solo/playing-alone.md).

![The Skyharbor sky map with seven island regions](/images/sky-map.svg)

## The game at a glance

The game lasts **eight rounds**. Each round has three phases:

- **Dawn:** reveal an event card and roll the wind die.
- **Action:** each captain takes three actions, one at a time.
- **Dusk:** pay your crew, refill the markets, and pass the first-captain token.

See [Round overview](/topics/turns/round-overview.md) for the details.

## What you do on your turn

Most turns follow a simple rhythm. You sail to an island, load the goods that are cheap there, and carry them to an island that pays more for them. Along the way, you complete contracts, hire crew members with special abilities, and build docks that earn you money every time another captain lands at them.

## How long is a round?

The first rounds go quickly, because everyone has only a few coins. Later rounds take longer, as captains plan longer routes and juggle more contracts.

> **Tip:** For a first game, consider the [Quick game](/topics/variants/quick-game.md) variant, which lasts six rounds.
