# How to Win {.concept}

At the end of the eighth round, the captain with the most **renown** wins.

You earn renown in five ways:

- **Contracts:** each contract card shows its renown value in the top corner.
- **Docks:** each dock you built is worth 2 renown.
- **Upgrades:** each upgrade on your airship board is worth 1 renown.
- **Crew sets:** one of each crew type (a full crew) is worth 4 renown.
- **Coins:** every 10 coins you have left are worth 1 renown.

Contracts are usually the largest source of renown, but a captain who ignores docks and upgrades rarely wins.

For the full list, see the [Scoring table](/topics/scoring/scoring-table.md). For ties, see [Breaking ties](/topics/scoring/breaking-ties.md).
