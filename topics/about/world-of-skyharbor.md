# The World of Skyharbor {.concept}

Long ago, the lowlands flooded and never dried. The people who survived built their towns on floating islands, held above the clouds by great crystals of lift-stone. Today, those islands depend on one another for everything: tea from the green terraces, silk from the weaver towns, spice from the southern reefs, and iron from the mining rocks.

Only airships can carry goods between the islands. You are the captain of one of them.

## Your goal

You start with a small, slow ship, a few coins, and a reputation that nobody has heard of yet. Over eight rounds, you buy cargo where it's cheap, sell it where it's wanted, and take on contracts that the island councils post at the harbors. Every contract you complete, dock you build, and upgrade you make earns **renown**.

## The sky is never calm

The wind changes every round, and storms drift across the map. A captain who reads the weather well can cross the sky in a single turn. A captain who doesn't may lose a whole hold of cargo.

When the eighth round ends, the captain with the most renown becomes Harbormaster of Skyharbor.
