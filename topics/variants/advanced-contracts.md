# Advanced Contracts {.reference}

The ten advanced contracts have a gold border. Shuffle them into the contract deck for a longer, more strategic game.

| Contract | Goods required | Deliver to | Renown | Coins |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Silk Road | 3 silk, 1 spice | Deepvein | 7 | 6 |
| Lanterns for the Festival | 2 iron, 2 silk | Brightspire | 6 | 5 |
| A Long Winter | 4 tea | Hollowrock | 6 | 4 |
| Spice for the Council | 3 spice | Loomhold | 5 | 6 |
| Rebuild the Docks | 3 iron | Windhollow | 5 | 3 |
| The Grand Feast | 1 of each good | Cloudmarket | 7 | 8 |
| Weavers' Order | 2 tea, 2 spice | Loomhold | 6 | 5 |
| Ironclad | 4 iron | Saltmere | 6 | 4 |
| Tea for the Miners | 3 tea, 1 silk | Deepvein | 5 | 5 |
| Emergency Supplies | 2 of any two goods | The storm's region | 7 | 10 |

> **Note:** "The storm's region" means the region next to the storm marker, since no ship can enter the storm itself.
