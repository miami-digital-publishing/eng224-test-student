# Cooperative Mode {.concept}

In cooperative mode, all captains work together to supply the islands before the great storm season arrives.

## The goal

Together, you must fulfill a number of contracts before the end of the eighth round. The number depends on how many captains are playing.

| Captains | Contracts needed |
|---|---|
| 2 | 10 |
| 3 | 13 |
| 4 | 16 |
| 5 | 18 |

## Changes to play

- Captains may give each other coins and cargo when their ships are in the same region.
- Docks don't charge landing fees.
- At every dusk, move the storm one region in the wind's direction, even if the die didn't show a storm.

## Losing

If the storm reaches Windhollow, or if the round tracker passes space 8 before you've fulfilled enough contracts, everyone loses.
